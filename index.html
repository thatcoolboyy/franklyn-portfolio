How I
work

Good design starts with people and ends with something an engineer can build. Here's what I bring to a team.

◎

I start with real people

Interviews, usability tests and A/B tests come before polished screens. I design for the person holding the phone, not for the Dribbble shot.

✂

I cut steps

Every flow I touch gets shorter and clearer. Whether it's a transfer, a skincare quiz or a food order, people should finish without help.

⚙

I speak developer

I studied Electronic Engineering and write HTML, CSS and JavaScript. My handoffs cover edge cases, accessibility and what happens when things go wrong.
